## Product

### DefiGate Payment Processor

A self-hosted, AI-powered cryptocurrency payment processor. Businesses accept digital asset payments securely and directly — with full control over funds, privacy, and infrastructure, and no third-party dependency.

One platform for decentralization, intelligent automation, and enterprise-grade security.

### Key Features

- **Zero transaction fees** — No gateway commissions or hidden processing costs.
- **Multi-currency support** — 20+ cryptocurrencies including BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, Solana, and Litecoin.
- **AI-powered dashboard** — Monitor transactions, analyze revenue, generate reports, and query a built-in AI assistant.
- **Self-hosted infrastructure** — Full ownership of the payment system, data, and private keys.
- **Automated processing** — Invoices, real-time confirmations, partial payments, and payouts.
- **Developer-friendly APIs** — REST APIs, webhooks, and plugins for WooCommerce, WHMCS, OpenCart, and more.
- **Security & privacy** — Non-custodial architecture, optional wallet encryption, 2FA, fraud detection, and full transaction transparency.
- **Flexible deployment** — Docker, Kubernetes, VPS, or cloud (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure).
